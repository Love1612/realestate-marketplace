# Phase 1 city launch checklist

Goal: prove RentHub in **one city** before national spend.

## 0. Decide

- [ ] Choose launch city (example candidates: St. Louis, Chicago, Dallas, Atlanta, Houston, Phoenix)
- [ ] Confirm primary renter email for ops: `dondejuan25@gmail.com`
- [ ] Confirm brand spelling **RentHub** on all Phase 1 creative

## 1. Product readiness (must ship)

- [ ] Live site healthy: https://rent-hub1.vercel.app
- [ ] Auth works (Google + magic link) for renters and owners
- [ ] Create listing → $5 Stripe Test Checkout → live listing
- [ ] Search + filters + map for the launch city
- [ ] Listing detail: photos, total monthly cost, amenities, availability
- [ ] Tours + messaging + favorites / saved searches
- [ ] Owner dashboard usable on mobile
- [ ] Supabase schema + RLS verified for Phase 1 tables
- [ ] Legal pages linked (privacy / fair housing baseline)

## 2. City supply (listings)

- [ ] Target: enough **live** listings to feel real (set a number, e.g. 25–50)
- [ ] Recruit 1–25 unit landlords first
- [ ] Message: “$5 to list” + “Fill vacancies faster — without paying big marketplace fees.”
- [ ] Owner share tools enabled (Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, copy link)
- [ ] “Powered by RentHub” on shared listing cards / pages

## 3. City demand (renters)

- [ ] Promise: “Find your next home without the hassle.”
- [ ] Local landing pages for the city (apartments, houses, pet-friendly, cheap, 2-bed, etc.)
- [ ] 5–10 short social videos for that city
- [ ] Trust creative: “Know before you go.”
- [ ] Track cost per qualified renter

## 4. Funnel instrumentation

- [ ] Renter: view → contact → tour → application
- [ ] Owner: signup → listing → lead → tour
- [ ] Weekly review of those metrics only (ignore vanity metrics)

## 5. Exit criteria to Phase 2 / next city

- [ ] Renters regularly view and contact listings
- [ ] Owners report usable leads
- [ ] At least one clear case study (anonymized OK)
- [ ] Playbook documented for city #2

## Non-goals for Phase 1

- National brand campaign
- Leading with “AI”
- Chasing large multifamily enterprise deals first
