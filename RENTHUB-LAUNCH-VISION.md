# RentHub launch vision

Brand spelling: always **RentHub** (capital R + H, one word).

## Core promises

**Renters:** Find your next home without the hassle.

**Owners:** Fill vacancies faster — without paying big marketplace fees.

## Campaign spine

**Rent smarter.**

- Real listings.
- Real prices.
- Real availability.
- Real options.

**Trust line:** Know before you go.

**Tagline set**

- Product: Find. List. Manage. Grow.
- Consumer: Your next home is waiting.
- Trust: Real listings. Clear prices. Easier renting.

Do **not** lead with “RentHub has AI.” Lead with: “Tell RentHub what you’re looking for.”

## Two-sided growth

### 1. Renters first (#1)

Renter traffic makes RentHub valuable to owners.

Promote five renter advantages:

1. Search exactly how you want — city, neighborhood, price, bedrooms, pets, parking, move-in date.
2. Search on the map — see what’s actually available around you.
3. Know the real monthly cost — no surprise fees.
4. See the actual property/unit — photos, video, floor plans, tours, amenities, availability.
5. Apply without the headache — search → save → tour → apply.

### 2. Small landlords second (1–25 properties)

Message: “You’re a landlord, not a marketing department.”

Simple offer: **$5 to list.**

Do not start by trying to pull giant operators off Zillow.

### 3. Then 25–500+ portfolio market

Property managers, communities, multifamily, investors, PM companies, realtors with rental inventory.

Pitch: “One platform for your entire rental portfolio.” Show the dashboard / SaaS story.

## Trust campaign

Sell confidence, not just listings:

- Verified listings
- Listing freshness
- Total monthly cost
- Exact-unit information
- Photos / video
- Tour scheduling
- Secure messaging
- Fraud / scam detection

## Channels (early)

- **Content-driven social** (TikTok / Reels / Facebook): local rent roundups, fee transparency, first-apartment tips, scam warnings → “Find it on RentHub.”
- **Local-city SEO:** own cities one at a time with landing pages (apartments, houses, pet-friendly, price bands, beds).
- **Owners as sales force:** share to Facebook / Instagram / X / LinkedIn / WhatsApp / copy link + “Powered by RentHub.”
- **Referrals early:** renters refer friends; owners refer landlords (e.g. next listing free).

Avoid dumping the whole budget into generic national ads before one launch market proves usage.

## Metrics that matter

Not downloads, impressions, or followers.

**Primary:** cost per qualified renter.

**Renter funnel:** Renter → Listing view → Contact → Tour → Application → Lease

**Owner funnel:** Owner signup → Listing → Lead → Tour → Lease

## Phased expansion

1. Own one city.
2. Fill it with great listings.
3. Drive renters to those listings.
4. Show owners that RentHub produces leads.
5. Use that proof to expand to the next city.
6. Convert successful owners into portfolio SaaS customers.
7. Expand nationally.

**Positioning:** not “another Zillow.”  
**Positioning:** the easier, clearer, more affordable way to rent — and the product must prove that every session.
